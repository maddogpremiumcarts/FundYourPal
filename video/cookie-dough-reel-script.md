# "The Cookie-Dough Trap" — 30-Second Reel Script

Instagram Reels and TikTok, written in the Fundraising Coach voice (see `docs/04-social-media-playbook.md`).

## Setup

Film vertically on a phone, held at chest height. The speaker is a coach or team parent in a team hoodie, standing at a home freezer stacked with fundraiser tubs. Use their real voice with burned-in captions, cut every 2–3 seconds, and keep trending audio very low or skip it.

## Script

| Time | Shot | On-screen text | Voiceover |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–3s | Freezer door swings open, tubs stacked to the top | "Last season's fundraiser 👀" | "This is last season's fundraiser. It's still in my freezer." |
| 3–8s | Quick cuts: order form, cash envelope, boxes in a garage | "Order forms. Cash. Delivery day." | "Cookie dough. Popcorn. Order forms, cash envelopes, and a whole weekend of deliveries." |
| 8–12s | Speaker faces camera, deadpan | "And you keep… part of it?" | "And after all that, your team keeps only part of every sale." |
| 12–19s | Phone screen showing a player's page with photo and goal bar | "Every player gets a page" | "Here's what works instead. Every player gets their own page, and families text it to Grandma once." |
| 19–25s | Donation notification pops up; progress bar moves | "Straight to your team's bank" | "Donations go straight to your team's bank. Nothing to sell, nothing to deliver, nothing to count." |
| 25–30s | Speaker shuts the freezer, same angle as the opening shot | "Comment DOUGH for the 3-text script" | "Comment 'dough' and I'll send you the exact texts to use." |

About 80 words, a comfortable pace for 30 seconds. Closing the freezer at the end mirrors the opening shot, so the video loops cleanly.

## Why it's built this way

- **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** A visual surprise (the stuffed freezer) plus a confession.
- **One idea only.** Pain, fix, one ask. Fees, the covered card fees and scholarships each get their own video.
- **Authority through a fix, not a pitch.** FundYourPal isn't named until the caption.
- **Comment-keyword call to action.** If you won't use automated DM replies, swap it for "Link in bio for the 3-text script."

## Before you film

The "still in my freezer" line has to be true for whoever is on camera. If it isn't their story, change it to "This is what most teams' fundraisers look like."
