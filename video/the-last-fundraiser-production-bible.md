# The Last Fundraiser: Production Bible

Oct 22, 2026

A 30-second satirical commercial for FundYourPal, prepared for AI video generation in Higgsfield.

## Overview

**Logline:** A weary team mom battles an endless season of cookie dough, cash envelopes and closed doors, shot like an epic war-movie trailer, until a record scratch reveals there's an easier way.

| Frame rate | 24 fps cinematic; slow-motion shots generated at high frame rate or slowed in edit |

### How to use this document with Higgsfield

1. **Create Denise first.** Generate one reference image from the master character prompt in the Casting section and reuse it in every shot.
2. **Generate one shot at a time** using the paste-ready prompts in the Shot-by-shot section. Each prompt is self-contained; paste it whole.
3. **Make 3–5 attempts per shot** and pick the best. Shots 1, 3 and 5 carry the comedy, so give them the most attempts.
4. **Add every word in the edit,** never in the AI generation. Logo, phone screens, captions and the call to action are layered on afterward.
5. **Record or generate the narrator separately,** then cut the picture to the voice.

### Rules for every shot

- No visible brand logos, team names, readable text or real people in AI frames
- Denise's face, hair and windbreaker must match the reference image
- Keep the subject centered so 9:16 and 16:9 crops both work
- Negative prompt for all shots: *text, letters, words, logos, watermark, extra fingers, distorted hands, warped faces, cartoon style, low resolution, flicker*

## Director's treatment

We are making a war movie about cookie dough. Every frame of the first 20 seconds is shot, lit and scored as if the fate of the world depends on one woman's clipboard. Nobody in the film knows it's funny. That is the joke.

**The rule of the satire:** treat the ordinary as mythic. A swinging garage bulb becomes an interrogation lamp. Cash envelopes fall like snow on a battlefield. A porch light switching off is a betrayal. A freezer opens like the Ark of a lost kingdom. We never wink; the audience does the laughing.

**The turn:** at 20 seconds a record scratch kills the score mid-swell. We cut to a quiet, sunlit kitchen, handheld and natural. Denise is in pajamas with coffee. Her phone buzzes. The epic world is gone; real life is easy now. The relief should feel physical.

### Performance direction for Denise

- **Shots 1–5:** stoic, weathered, haunted. Think of a soldier on her fourth tour. Minimal expression, maximum intensity. Jaw set. Slow blinks.
- **Shot 6:** completely human. A small, surprised, genuine smile. Shoulders drop. She almost laughs.

### Beat map

| Time | Beat | Emotional temperature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–4s | The call: one parent is chosen | Ominous |
| 4–8s | The reluctant hero | Grim resolve |
| 8–12s | The battle: cash envelopes | Chaos, slow motion |
| 12–16s | The betrayal: the porch light | Lonely, rain-soaked |
| 16–20s | The abyss: the freezer is still full | Operatic despair |
| 20–24s | Record scratch. The easier way | Warm relief |
| 24–30s | The answer: FundYourPal | Calm, confident |

### Pacing

Shots 1–5 are slow and heavy, letting the narrator's pauses land. Shot 6 is the only shot with real-time, natural motion. The end card holds for a full 5–6 seconds so people can act on it.

## Casting

### Denise (lead, on screen in every shot)

The audience has to see themselves in her in half a second. She is not a model; she is the mom running the team group chat.

| Attribute | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Age | Early to mid 40s |
| Build | Average, athletic-ish; someone who carries boxes and folding chairs |
| Face | Warm, expressive, lightly tired; faint smile lines; minimal makeup |
| Hair | Shoulder-length, medium brown with a few lighter strands, pulled into a slightly messy ponytail with loose pieces around the face |
| Ethnicity | Open; choose one and keep it identical in every shot |
| Energy | Capable, funny, quietly exhausted; reads as "the one who always ends up doing it" |
| Avoid | Glamour, heavy makeup, perfect hair, overly young or catalog-model looks |

### Master character reference prompt (generate this image first)

> Photorealistic portrait of a woman in her early 40s, a youth sports team mom, warm expressive face with faint smile lines and slightly tired eyes, minimal natural makeup, shoulder-length medium brown hair in a slightly messy ponytail with loose strands framing her face, wearing a navy blue nylon windbreaker jacket with white piping and no logos, a gray hoodie underneath, a silver coach's whistle on a red lanyard around her neck. Neutral background, soft even daylight, 85mm portrait lens, shallow depth of field, cinematic realism.

Save the best result as **Denise\_REF** and attach it to every shot as the character reference.

### The narrator (voice only)

- **Shots 1–5:** a deep, gravelly, gravely serious male movie-trailer voice; slow delivery with long pauses. Never smiles. Generic "epic trailer" style; don't imitate a specific real voice actor.
- **Shots 6–7:** the same narrator dropping the act, or a warm, friendly female voice in her 30s–40s that sounds like a fellow team parent. Recommended: switch voices; the contrast sells the turn.

### Background figures

- **Shot 4:** no neighbor is seen. Only a silhouette behind a frosted door window, or nothing at all; the porch light does the acting.
- **Grandma:** never on screen. She appears only as a donation notification in shot 6, added in the edit.

## Production design

Ordinary suburban spaces, art-directed as if they were battlefields. Everything must feel real and lived-in; the drama comes from light, smoke and scale, not fantasy.

## Cinematography

The first five shots borrow the grammar of a big-budget war epic: slow push-ins, heroic low angles, hard backlight, haze and anamorphic flares. The last two drop to the grammar of real life: handheld, eye-level, natural window light.

### Camera and lenses

| Shot | Lens | Angle | Movement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 24mm wide, anamorphic look | Low, eye-level with boxes | Slow dolly push-in |
| 2 | 85mm | Low angle, extreme close-up | Locked off, imperceptible push |
| 3 | 35mm | Medium, slightly low | Slow-motion, gentle orbit |
| 4 | 50mm | Medium-wide from behind, then side profile | Slow track following her |
| 5 | 18mm wide | Top-down crane | Crane up and away |
| 6 | 35mm | Eye level | Handheld, natural micro-movement |
| 7 | n/a | Graphic end card | Static |

### Lighting

| Shot | Key light | Mood |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Single swinging bare bulb overhead, hard and warm; cool blue dusk through the garage window; haze catching the light | Ominous interrogation |
| 2 | Hard warm side light, deep shadow on the far side of her face | Grim resolve |
| 3 | Strong backlight from the pendant lamp, envelopes glowing as they fall; dark kitchen | Battlefield chaos |
| 4 | Sodium-orange streetlamp backlight, cold blue fill, rain catching light; warm porch light switches off | Noir loneliness |
| 5 | Cold white glow rising from inside the freezer, almost heavenly; mist pouring out | Operatic despair |
| 6 | Soft golden morning sun through the window, natural bounce | Warm relief |

### Look and grade

- **Shots 1–5:** teal-and-orange blockbuster grade, crushed blacks, light film grain, anamorphic horizontal lens flares, 2.39:1 letterbox feel inside the frame (optional black bars in 16:9)
- **Shots 6–7:** clean, bright, natural color; no letterbox, no flares, minimal grain. The sudden openness of the frame is part of the relief.

### Slow motion

Shots 3 and 5 play at roughly 40% speed. In Higgsfield, describe the action as "in slow motion"; if the result is too fast, slow it in the edit with frame blending.

## Shot-by-shot

Each prompt below is complete on its own. Attach **Denise\_REF** as the character reference for shots 1–6 and paste the shared negative prompt from the Overview.

### Shot 1: "The Chosen One" (0:00–0:04)

**Paste-ready prompt:**

> Cinematic wide shot inside a suburban two-car garage at dusk, a single bare light bulb swinging slowly on a cord, low fog drifting across the concrete floor, slow dolly push-in toward a towering floor-to-ceiling wall of plain brown cardboard boxes stacked like a fortress. A woman in her early 40s with a messy brown ponytail, navy windbreaker with white piping, gray hoodie and a whistle on a red lanyard stands small in silhouette before the wall, clutching a clipboard. Cool blue dusk light from a side window, warm hard light from the bulb, haze catching light beams, anamorphic lens flare, teal and orange color grade, epic ominous war-movie trailer mood, photorealistic, 24mm lens, 24fps.

- **Action:** Denise stands perfectly still; only the bulb and fog move.
- **Narrator:** "Every season... one parent is chosen."
- **Sound:** Low cinematic drone, a deep boom on "chosen."
- **Edit:** Hold 4 seconds. Start on black for 6 frames, fade up with the drone.

### Shot 2: "The Reluctant Hero" (0:04–0:08)

**Paste-ready prompt:**

> Extreme close-up, low angle, of a woman in her early 40s with a messy brown ponytail and loose strands of hair blowing gently, tired determined eyes slowly narrowing, faint smile lines, minimal makeup, navy windbreaker collar and red lanyard visible at the bottom of frame, clutching a curled clipboard of papers to her chest. Hard warm side light, deep shadow on the other half of her face, dark garage background with soft bokeh of a bare bulb, slight haze, heroic and grim, cinematic war-movie close-up, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, teal and orange grade, photorealistic.

- **Action:** A slow blink, eyes narrow, jaw tightens. No smile.
- **Narrator:** "She did not volunteer. She simply... didn't say no fast enough."
- **Sound:** Strings rising underneath; wind whoosh.
- **Edit:** Let the pause before "didn't say no" breathe. Cut on her eyes narrowing.

### Shot 3: "The Envelope Storm" (0:08–0:12)

**Paste-ready prompt:**

> Slow-motion medium shot in a dark family kitchen at night, dozens of plain white and manila paper envelopes and loose paper bills swirling through the air like falling snow, coins bouncing off the counter, a woman in her early 40s with a messy brown ponytail, navy windbreaker with white piping, gray hoodie and red lanyard lunging forward to grab them, a smudge of dust on her cheek, intense focused face. Strong backlight from a single pendant lamp making the envelopes glow, the rest of the kitchen in shadow, gentle orbiting camera, epic battlefield energy, anamorphic flare, teal and orange grade, photorealistic, 35mm lens.

- **Action:** Denise reaches through the falling paper, catches one envelope, more keep falling.
- **Narrator:** "She has counted... the cash envelopes."
- **Sound:** Orchestra builds; coins ringing in slow motion; muffled heartbeat.
- **Edit:** Speed ramp: normal for 6 frames, then slow to 40%.

### Shot 4: "The Betrayal" (0:12–0:16)

**Paste-ready prompt:**

> Cinematic night shot in heavy rain on a quiet suburban driveway, a woman in her early 40s with her navy windbreaker hood up, soaked hair, carrying a tall stack of plain brown cardboard boxes toward a front door with a frosted glass panel and a warm porch light. As she reaches the top step, the porch light suddenly switches off and she is left in darkness. Sodium-orange streetlamp backlight behind her, cold blue fill, rain streaks catching the light, wet pavement reflections, lonely noir mood, slow tracking camera following from behind then settling on her profile, 50mm lens, photorealistic.

- **Action:** She stops. Water drips from her hood. She stares at the dark door.
- **Narrator:** "She has faced... the neighbors."
- **Sound:** Rain; a sharp thunder crack timed exactly to the light switching off.
- **Edit:** Cut to the thunder.

### Shot 5: "The Freezer" (0:16–0:20)

**Paste-ready prompt:**

> Overhead crane shot in a dim suburban garage, an old white chest freezer lid rising slowly on its own, thick icy mist pouring out over the edges, an intense heavenly white light glowing up from inside revealing it packed to the brim with round plastic tubs with blank white lids. A woman in her early 40s with a messy brown ponytail, navy windbreaker with white piping and red lanyard sinks slowly to her knees beside it in despair, arms falling to her sides. Camera cranes up and away, operatic and tragic, slow motion, haze, cool white light against dark surroundings, epic war-movie finale mood, 18mm lens, photorealistic.

- **Action:** The lid rises, light floods out, she kneels.
- **Narrator (whispered):** "And still... the freezer... remains full."
- **Sound:** A choir swells to a peak, then a **record scratch** at 0:19.5 and total silence.
- **Edit:** Cut to black for 4 frames on the record scratch before shot 6.

### Shot 6: "The Easier Way" (0:20–0:24)

**Paste-ready prompt:**

> Bright warm sunlit family kitchen in the morning, clean tidy counters, a bowl of oranges and a green plant on the windowsill, golden sunlight streaming in. A woman in her early 40s with a loose brown ponytail wearing an oversized cream sweatshirt and plaid flannel pajama pants, holding a steaming ceramic mug, glances down at a smartphone in her other hand as it buzzes; her face softens into a surprised, genuine, relieved smile and her shoulders relax. Handheld natural camera at eye level, soft natural light, realistic everyday color, no lens flare, cozy and calm, 35mm lens, photorealistic.

- **Action:** Phone buzzes, she looks, small smile, almost a laugh.
- **Narrator (warm, friendly voice):** "There is an easier way."
- **Sound:** Birdsong, coffee-mug clink, phone buzz; soft acoustic guitar begins.
- **Edit:** Composite a real FundYourPal screenshot onto the phone screen in a 1-second insert: a player page with a photo, goal bar and a "Grandma gave $50" notification.

### Shot 7: End card (0:24–0:30)

No AI generation needed. Built in the edit; see the next sections.

- **Narrator:** "Fill out our form to get started."
- **Sound:** The acoustic guitar resolves; a soft freezer-door thunk as the button appears.

## Sound and music

Sound does half the comedy. The score must be played completely straight, as if for a real blockbuster, so the record scratch lands like a slap.

## Edit, text and delivery

### Assembly order

1. Lay the narration track first, at the timings in the Sound section.
2. Drop shots 1–6 onto the voice; trim each to its window.
3. Add the music bed and hit the record scratch at exactly 0:19.5.
4. Insert 4 frames of black after the scratch.
5. Composite the real FundYourPal phone screenshot into shot 6.
6. Build the end card, then add captions throughout.
7. Grade shots 1–5 teal and orange; leave 6–7 clean and warm.

### On-screen text

Add all text in the editor; never generate it with AI.

| Time | Text | Style |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Burned-in captions of the narration | Bold white serif in the style of an epic movie title, centered lower third |
| 0:20–0:24 | Captions switch style | Clean, friendly sans-serif, sentence case |
| 0:24–0:27 | "Every player gets a page. Donations go straight to your team's bank." | End card, two lines |
| 0:27–0:30 | "Fill out our form to get started." plus a button graphic and the FundYourPal logo | End card, button pops in with the freezer thunk |

### End card layout

- FundYourPal logo top center
- Supporting line in the middle
- A large, high-contrast call-to-action button in the lower third reading "Get started"
- Denise's mug in soft focus at the bottom edge
- Keep everything inside the platform safe zone: nothing in the top 14% or bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame, where app buttons and captions cover the screen
