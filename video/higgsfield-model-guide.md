# Which Higgsfield Models to Use for "The Last Fundraiser"

Notes as of October 2026. The model lineup changes often; check what your plan offers.

## Step 1: Create Denise as a still image first

Generate her reference portrait (see the Casting section of `the-last-fundraiser-production-bible.md`) and a starting frame for each shot with Higgsfield's image models (Soul or Nano Banana Pro). Then animate those frames with image-to-video. Starting every shot from an image of her keeps her face consistent across shots.

Skip Soul ID for this project: it trains a character from 20+ reference photos of a real person, and Denise is invented.

## Step 2: Match the model to the shot

| Shots | What matters | Model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 (close-up of her eyes), 6 (morning smile) | Facial expression and natural human motion | Kling (newest version on your plan) |
| 1 (garage), 3 (envelope storm), 4 (rain), 5 (freezer) | Lighting, atmosphere, epic camera moves | Veo 3.1, run through Cinema Studio for camera control |
| Anything that won't come out right | A second opinion | Seedance |

## Step 3: Draft cheap, finish expensive

Test each prompt on a cheaper model or draft mode until the framing works, then render the keeper on Veo or Kling. Premium models cost far more per clip.

## Lineup reference (October 2026)

One roundup lists Kling 4.0, Seedance 2.5, WAN 3.0, Veo 3.1, Hailuo 2.3, Cinema Studio 4.0, HappyHorse, Grok Imagine 1.5 and Gemini Omni Flash, with the full set available from the Plus plan up. Much of the "best for consistency" material online comes from Higgsfield's own marketing, so judge by your test renders.
