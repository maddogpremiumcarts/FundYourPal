## Before you send

These emails work because they come from a real director talking to peers, not from a software company. Send them as plain emails from your own account, in your own voice.

- The price is now **10% total**. FundYourPal pays the Stripe card processing fees, and donors can choose to cover the 10% so the team gets 100%.
- Email 3 now leads with "we even cover the card fees," the strongest new reason to believe.

### Say you're involved

If you have any connection to FundYourPal beyond being a customer (you work on it, own part of it, or get paid for referrals), say so. Coaches trust you more for it, and endorsement rules require it. Email 1 has a one-line disclosure to keep or adjust.

### Who to send to

- Head coaches and directors in your league, conference and rival programs
- Booster club presidents and team parents you know
- Coaches from other sports at your school or club
- People who'd recognize your name

Stick to people who know you or your program. Bulk cold email from a personal account can land in spam and hurt your account.
