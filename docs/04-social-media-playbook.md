# FundYourPal Social Media Playbook

Oct 22, 2026

## The voice: "The Fundraising Coach"

Our audience doesn't want a software company. They want **the veteran team parent who has run every fundraiser, made every mistake, and now tells you exactly what works.** Think of the coach at the end of the bench who has seen 14 seasons, still loves it, and won't waste your time.

### Where the authority comes from

- **Receipts, not claims.** Real numbers, real teams, the fee math shown out loud.
- **Scar tissue.** "I've sold the cookie dough. I've counted the cash envelopes." Experience earns the right to advise.
- **Systems.** Gives a plan ("the 3-text ask," "the 21-day season fund") instead of tips.
- **Honesty.** Admits what doesn't work, including when a free tool is fine.

### Where the relatability comes from

- **Confession first.** Opens with a shared pain before giving advice.
- **Sideline humor.** The garage fridge, the same three volunteers, the hotel block you're forced to book.
- **On their side.** Protective of parents, kids and grandparents; never preachy about "giving."
- **Their words.** Travel ball, comp season, team mom, the group chat.

### The balance

| Too far toward "friend" | The sweet spot | Too far toward "expert" |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Ugh, fundraising is the worst, am I right?? 😭" | "Fundraising is the worst part of the season. It doesn't have to take more than an evening. Here's how." | "Optimize your peer-to-peer fundraising conversion funnel." |
| Commiserates, offers nothing | Names the pain, then gives a fix | Gives the fix, never shows they've felt the pain |

### Voice formula for every post

**Name the pain in their words → share a quick story or number → give one clear fix → soft call to action.**

### Content mix

- 70% genuinely useful (scripts, plans, math, sponsor tips) whether or not they ever use FundYourPal
- 20% stories and proof (real teams, grandparents, coaches)
- 10% direct product posts ("Start in 5 minutes")

## Topic 1: The cookie-dough trap

**The pain:** Product sales eat weekends, fill garages, embarrass families, and keep only part of every sale. Everyone hates them; everyone keeps doing them because "that's what we've always done."

### Quote bank (their words)

- "embarrassed to show the catalogs to the grandparents" — a parent on Reddit ([Scary Mommy](https://www.scarymommy.com/parenting/school-unfundraiser-reddit))
- "Then why don't they just ask me for money" — a forum member flooded with kids' sales ([Cathe forum](https://www.cathe.com/forum/threads/flooded-by-all-different-kinds-of-donation-requests.226471))
- "I'm not, as a mom and a family, set up to do that" — a mom on pitching, collecting and delivering ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/parents-balking-at-school-fundraising))

### Words to use

cookie dough, popcorn, wrapping paper, candles, order forms, freezer full, garage fridge, delivery day, overpriced, junk, "nothing to sell," "nothing to deliver"

### Post ideas

| Hook | Format | Fix you give |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "This is last year's fundraiser." (opens freezer) | 10-second video | Swap to a no-sell fundraiser |
| "I added up what our popcorn sale really earned. Then I added up the hours." | Carousel | Count volunteer hours, not just dollars |
| "5 fundraisers parents secretly hate (and 1 they don't)" | List post | The no-sell page as #1 |

## Topic 2: The same three parents

**The pain:** A tiny group of volunteers, usually moms, carries the fundraising for everyone. They're exhausted, quietly resentful, and scared that if they stop, the season falls apart.

### Quote bank (their words)

- "Six or eight of us do all the work" — a booster club secretary, out of 1,400 families ([HeraldNet](https://www.heraldnet.com/?p=264737))
- "the same ten people" — the running PTA joke about who does everything ([Seattle's Child](https://www.seattleschild.com/how-to-fight-school-volunteer-burnout/))
- "Debbie Do Everything" — a booster club podcast's name for the volunteer who carries it all ([BoosterHub](https://boosterhub.com/podcast/how-to-recruit-more-volunteers-and-keep-them-coming-back))
- A Texas baseball mom described team fundraising as a second job on top of her full-time one ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/wbna32574456)) *(paraphrased)*

### Words to use

team mom, team parent, booster board, the group chat, "the same three of us," volunteer, burnout, second job, "I got stuck with fundraising," "nobody else signed up"

### Post ideas

| Hook | Format | Fix you give |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Raise your hand if you're the team mom who got stuck with fundraising." | Talking-head video | A 30-minute setup that runs itself |
| "Every team has a Debbie Do Everything. This one's for her." | Text post | Hand off the work: each family shares once |
| "How to run a fundraiser without one parent doing it all" | Carousel | The "share once" rule plus a 3-text script |

## Topic 3: Where the money goes

**The pain:** Coaches and parents suspect that someone in the middle keeps a big cut, and donors feel nickel-and-dimed at checkout. Treasurers feel watched after booster-theft headlines. This topic is your strongest, because FundYourPal never holds the money.

### Quote bank (their words)

- "This is a rip off" — a coach after reading how online fundraising fees work ([WCHS Insight](https://wchsinsight.org/24530/opinion/online-fundraisers-revolutionary-or-ripoff/))
- "another way to extract money from donors" — a donor on surprise checkout add-ons ([Donorbox](https://donorbox.org/nonprofit-blog/snap-raise-reviews))
- "The more you raise the more you pay" — a reviewer on percentage pricing ([Serchen](https://www.serchen.com/company/snap-raise))
- "no reason for schools to give away money raised by parents" — a critic of platform cuts ([Willamette Week](https://wweek.com/news/2022/04/06/portland-high-school-football-boosters-are-using-a-seattle-software-the-school-district-banned))

### Words to use

middleman, cut, fee, "10% fee," donors can cover the card fees, no monthly bill, straight to your team's bank, every dollar recorded, treasurer, receipts, no surprise charges, no pre-checked add-ons, "the more you raise, the less you pay"

### Post ideas

| Hook | Format | Fix you give |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "We raised $20,000. Here's what we actually kept." | Fee-math carousel | Compare 10% (and 7% above $25,000) to a 20% cut |
| "3 questions to ask before any fundraising app touches your team's money" | Checklist post | Where does money land? What's the total fee? Any add-ons? |
| "Your treasurer deserves better than a shoebox of cash envelopes." | Short video | Direct-to-bank, every donation recorded |

Stay factual when naming competitors: quote published fees, use "up to," and date the comparison. Let the math do the talking; never call a rival a scam.

## Topic 4: No kid sits out

**The pain:** Youth sports costs keep climbing, and coaches watch talented kids drift away because of dues, gear and travel. Many coaches quietly cover those kids themselves. This topic carries the most emotion and wins over donors who are tired of "pay for our trip" asks.

### Quote bank (their words)

- "Youth sports inflation is out of control" — Tom Farrey, Aspen Institute ([Project Play](https://projectplay.org/news/2025/2/24/project-play-survey-family-spending-on-youth-sports-rises-46-over-five-years))
- "Sports is now pricing kids out" — a youth sports advocate in Ohio ([Spectrum News](https://spectrumnews1.com/oh/toledo/news/2026/01/11/youth-sports-rising-costs-ohio))
- "I feel like the joy of youth sports has been corroded" — quoted in the Washington Post ([Post Guam](https://www.postguam.com/sports/nation/the-soaring-price-of-youth-sports/article_d896f17d-ae41-4758-99fb-1e79fdac7baf.html))
- "If you can't pay anything, you still play" — a rec program leader ([MLB.com](https://mlb.com/news/reds-community-fund-youth-sports-2024-givingtuesday))

### Proof points to pair with it

- Families spend about $1,016 a year on a child's main sport, up 46% since 2019 (Project Play)
- 20% of parents said money worries made them cut back or drop a sport (New York Life survey, via the Washington Post)

### Words to use

no kid sits out, dues, team fees, gear, travel, scholarship, fee assistance, "quietly," "every kid plays," "talent isn't the problem, money is"

### Post ideas

| Hook | Format | Fix you give |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Dues went up 46%. Talent didn't." | Stat graphic | Add a scholarship fund to your team page |
| "Every coach has a kid they quietly cover. Here's how to stop paying out of pocket." | Story post | A private scholarship fund donors can give to |
| "How to ask for help for a kid without embarrassing their family" | Carousel | Fund the team pool, never name the family |

## Topic 5: Asking without begging

**The pain:** Parents feel awkward asking family, coworkers and friends for money, and donors are tired of being spammed and guilt-tripped. Teach families how to ask with dignity, and show donors a page they're happy to give on.

### Quote bank (their words)

- "I find the fundraising embarrassing but I can't buy my way out of it." — a parent ([Berkeley Parents Network](https://www.berkeleyparentsnetwork.org/questions/awkward-situation-constantly-fundraising-family))
- "I am not buying from your kids" — a coworker's blanket rule ([SavingAdvice](https://www.savingadvice.com/forums/forum/financial-chit-chat/general-discussion/65637-?p=551018))
- "felt a little squeamish going back to the well" — a sportswriter and travel-ball parent with a small family ([Good Game Kid](https://goodgamekid.substack.com/p/gg-galore-should-rich-youth-sports))
- "want and deserve to hear from you, personally" — a student on how donors should be asked ([LOHS Lakeviews student paper](https://lohslakeviews.com/1344/opinions/snapraise-sucks/))
- A donor reported about 20 spam emails within three days of giving once ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/snapraise.com)) *(paraphrased)*

### Words to use

awkward, begging, spam, "share once," "just family," Grandma, Grandpa, aunties, "no account needed," "no pressure," "cheer them on," "their own page," "a quick text"

### Post ideas

| Hook | Format | Fix you give |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "The 3-text ask that gets grandparents to give (without sounding like a sales pitch)" | Carousel with scripts | Copy-and-paste texts parents can send |
| "Grandma lives 900 miles away. She's still on the team." | 15-second video | The per-kid page with photo, goal and schedule |
| "Your coworkers don't want popcorn. Your family wants to cheer on your kid." | Text post | Skip the office; share the kid's page with family |

## Word bank for posts

Keep this open while writing. If a word isn't on the left, ask whether a team parent would say it at the field.

| Category | Use these | Never these |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The job | fundraiser, raise money, cover the season, the trip fund | campaign, monetize, revenue, initiative |
| The people | players, athletes, kids, dancers, team mom, team parent, coach, booster board, Grandma | participants, users, stakeholders, donors (in parent-facing posts) |
| The money | 10% fee, donors can cover the card fees, no monthly bill, straight to your team's bank, the more you raise the less you pay | disbursement, platform fee structure, processing rate |
| The feeling | finally, done, easy, proud, no pressure, no selling, relief | seamless, empower, revolutionize, game-changer |
| Their world | travel ball, 12U, comp season, the bid, nationals, Cooperstown, Worlds, tournament fees, hotel block, the group chat | the program year, extracurricular engagement |
| The enemy | cookie dough, order forms, cash envelopes, spam, surprise charges, the middleman | competitor names in a mocking way |

### Hook starters that fit the voice

- "Raise your hand if..."
- "I've run every fundraiser there is. Here's the only one I'd run again."
- "Nobody tells new team moms this..."
- "Here's the math on..."
- "Stop doing \[thing\]. Do this instead."
- "The 3-text ask..." / "The 5-minute setup..."
- "If your fundraiser still \[pain\], it's not working."

## Ready-to-post examples

Five drafts, one per topic. Bracketed items need your real details.

**1. The cookie-dough trap (Facebook / Instagram caption)**

> I've run every fundraiser there is. Cookie dough. Mulch. Raffles. Car washes in July.
>
> The worst? Sixty tubs of dough nobody picked up, sitting in a garage fridge.
>
> Here's what I'd do instead: give every player their own page, have families text it to Grandma once, and let the money go straight to your team's bank.
>
> Nothing to sell. Nothing to deliver. Nothing to count.

**2. The same three parents (Instagram Reel script)**

> On screen: "Raise your hand if you're the team mom who got stuck with fundraising." Voice: "You're not alone. On most teams, three parents do everything. So here's the rule: every family shares their kid's page once. That's it. Setup takes 5 minutes, and you're done carrying it."

**3. Where the money goes (carousel)**

> Slide 1: "We raised $\[amount\]. Here's what we actually kept." Slide 2: "3 questions before any app touches your team's money: Where does it land? What's the fee, total? Any add-ons at checkout?" Slide 3: "Our answers: your team's bank. A 10% fee plus card processing, and donors can cover both. No pre-checked add-ons." Slide 4: "Raise past $25,000? Your rate drops to 7%. The more you raise, the less you pay."

**4. No kid sits out (text post)**

> Every coach has a kid they quietly cover.
>
> Dues went up. Travel went up. Talent didn't change.
>
> Add a scholarship fund to your team page. Donors give to the pool, no family gets named, and no kid sits out over money.

**5. Asking without begging (carousel of scripts)**

> Text 1: "Hi Grandma! I made the \[team\] this season. Here's my page with my goal: \[link\]" Text 2 (a week later): "We're halfway to our goal! Thank you for cheering me on." Text 3: "We did it! Here's a photo from \[tournament\]." Caption: "Three texts. No catalog. No spam. Just family cheering on a kid."

### Weekly rhythm

| Day | Topic | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | Where the money goes | Fee math or checklist |
| Tuesday | The same three parents | Relatable video |
| Wednesday | Asking without begging | Scripts and templates |
| Thursday | No kid sits out | Story or stat |
| Friday | The cookie-dough trap | Humor, then the fix |
| Weekend | Proof | A real team, a goal hit, a grandparent's gift |

## Before you post

- First-person stories (like the sixty tubs of dough) come from our fictional Coach Ben. Post them only in the voice of a real coach telling their own story, or reframe as "a coach told us..." once it's true.
- Confirm "no pre-checked add-ons," "donors can cover the card fees" and the 7% tier match the product before using them.
- Quotes in the quote bank are for your research and inspiration. If you put one in a post, keep it short, credit the source, and don't present it as a FundYourPal customer.
